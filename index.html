About

I am a lecturer at Raffles University, where I teach programming, neural networks and computer vision, supervise postgraduate research (PhD and Master's), and develop curriculum. My research spans visual inspection, face analysis, self-supervised learning and AI for education.

Research Interests

Tiny Object Detection

Detecting small objects reliably, benchmarked against state-of-the-art detectors.

Visual Defect Inspection

Automated quality inspection for food and snack production lines.

Face Image Quality & Emotion

Face image quality assessment and contrastive self-supervised learning for facial emotion recognition.

AI in Education

AI coaching and simulation systems that help students build professional skills.
