Current Students

PhD

AI-Based Counselling Skills Simulator and Coach

Jayson Lew Chi Kheong

A culturally grounded AI client simulator that helps Malaysian counselling students practise and get feedback.

Read more

This research proposes a culturally grounded AI-powered counselling simulation and assessment framework to enhance practical counsellor education in Malaysia through realistic client interactions that incorporate local languages, cultural values, emotional complexity and diverse client behaviours. The framework integrates automated counselling micro-skill recognition, personalised formative feedback and human supervisor oversight, with its effectiveness evaluated through expert assessments and comparisons of students' counselling competencies, self-efficacy and transfer of skills to real-world practice.

Anomaly Detection for Industry Use Case

Yian Xing Jian

Real-time detection of defects and abnormal conditions from industrial sensor, image and video data.

Read more

This research proposes an AI-powered anomaly detection framework for industrial applications, leveraging machine learning and deep learning techniques to identify unusual patterns, defects and abnormal operating conditions from industrial sensor data, images or video streams. The framework aims to improve operational efficiency, predictive maintenance and quality control by providing accurate, real-time anomaly detection, early warning capabilities and interpretable insights to support industrial decision-making.

Master's

Face Quality Assessment using Self Supervised Learning

Mohamad Hatim Bin Mohd Shahfri

Learning face image quality without costly human labels, for robust real-world face recognition.

Read more

This research proposes a self-supervised learning framework for Face Image Quality Assessment (FIQA), aiming to learn meaningful facial image quality representations without relying heavily on expensive human-annotated quality labels. The framework seeks to improve the accuracy, generalisation and robustness of face quality prediction across different imaging conditions and face recognition systems, particularly in resource-constrained and real-world deployment scenarios.
