About

I am a senior lecturer at Raffles University, where I teach programming, neural networks and computer vision, supervise postgraduate research (PhD and Master's), and develop curriculum. I am also head of Data and AI Center of Excellence (DAICoE) and the research spans visual inspection, face sensing application, self-supervised learning and AI for education.

Research Interests

Video Content Analysis

An AI-powered application for automated video analysis, object detection, activity recognition, and intelligent event understanding.

Human-centric Vision and Biometrics

An intelligent AI application for human perception, biometric recognition, behavioural analysis, and identity verification.

Generative AI

An intelligent generative AI application for facial image analysis, enhancement, and 3D realistic face generation.

Medical Imaging

An AI-powered application for medical image analysis, abnormality detection, and intelligent diagnostic support.

Efficient and Scalable Vision

An efficient and scalable vision AI solution for real-time visual intelligence, resource-efficient inference, and deployment across edge and cloud environments.
